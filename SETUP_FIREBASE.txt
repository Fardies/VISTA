WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES AND DOES NOT DO
- Same VISTA website, two pages, one shared Firestore database.
- Real-time operator list, login, visitor check-in/out, manual checkout, CSV.
- Visitor checkout requires the SAME browser/session as check-in.
- Operators can manually check out visitors who switch devices.
- No offline mode, visitor identity verification, retention/deletion workflow,
  or rate-limiting / abuse protection. Anonymous visitors can submit fake records.
- For real personal data, obtain organizational approval and implement a
  privacy notice, retention/deletion policy, backups, and operational security
  review first. The app is FUNCTIONAL after setup but not security-certified.
- Firebase Spark free quotas apply; no payment needed within those limits.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- Access denied: rules not published, wrong email, or not signed in.
- Check-in denied: rules not published or Anonymous provider disabled.
- Zero visitors: create a test visit and check internet / browser console.
- Operator login works but dashboard denied: verify the rules in the live
  Firebase Console, not just your local firestore.rules file.
