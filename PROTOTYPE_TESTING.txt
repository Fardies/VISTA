VISTA — LOCAL FACE MATCHING (Spark plan, free)
==============================================

What this is
  Face check-in/check-out with Human.js that runs on the free Spark plan.
  - Face templates are saved ONLY in the guard phone's browser (localStorage).
  - Nothing biometric is uploaded to Firestore. Visits are updated as normal,
    with checkInMethod / checkOutMethod = "face-local".
  - Check-in and check-out MUST be on the same phone and the same browser.
  - Templates are deleted at check-out or after 24 hours.
  - Test only with yourself and coworkers who agreed. Use test names
    (e.g. "IZZAH TEST"), not real visitors.

Mode switch (face-scanner.js, top of file)
  const FACE_MODE = 'local';   // Local face matching on Spark (now)
  const FACE_MODE = 'cloud';   // Cloud Functions on Blaze (later, see FACE_SETUP.txt)
  The Cloud Functions code in functions/ is kept unchanged.


1. ONE-TIME: PUBLISH THE RULES (Farah, free on Spark)
-----------------------------------------------------
Firebase Console > Firestore Database > Rules > paste firestore.rules > Publish.
Without this, Register Visit or Confirm will show "Permission denied".
Do NOT upgrade Firebase or deploy functions for this prototype.


2. TEST ON THE LAPTOP FIRST (easiest)
-------------------------------------
  npx serve -l 5501 --no-clipboard
  Visitor:  http://localhost:5501
  Guard:    http://localhost:5501/guard.html
The laptop webcam works on localhost. Use the front camera ("Flip" button).


3. TEST ON YOUR PHONE (needs an https link)
-------------------------------------------
Phones only open the camera on https://. A local IP like http://192.168.x.x
will NOT work. Free option, no account needed (Cloudflare quick tunnel):
  Terminal 1:  npx serve -l 5501 --no-clipboard
  Terminal 2:  npx cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:5501
Terminal 2 prints a link like https://something-words.trycloudflare.com.
Open <that link>/guard.html on the phone. Keep both terminals open.
(First run downloads the cloudflared program, about 30 MB.)
Note: the link changes every time; anyone with the link can open the page,
so close the tunnel (Ctrl+C) when you finish testing.

Android (Chrome)
  - Tap Allow when asked for the camera.
  - If blocked: tap the icon left of the address > Permissions > Camera > Allow.

iPhone (Safari)
  - Use Safari, iOS 15 or newer. Do NOT use Private Browsing: it deletes the
    saved faces when the tab closes.
  - Safari may ask for camera permission on every visit. To stop it:
    Settings > Apps > Safari > Camera > Allow.
  - Switching to another app stops the camera; just tap Scan Again.
  - Safari can delete site data if the site is not opened for about 7 days.
  - Older iPhones scan more slowly; keep the phone steady for 2-3 seconds.
Both
  - First open downloads about 10 MB of face models (use Wi-Fi).
  - Good light on the face; no strong light behind the person.


4. FULL WORKFLOW TEST
---------------------
  a) Phone or laptop: open the visitor page, Register Visit as "IZZAH TEST",
     tick "I agree to face recognition", Submit.
  b) Guard phone: guard.html > sign in > Face Scanner tab.
  c) Under Waiting for Check-In, press Scan Face on IZZAH TEST > Start Scan.
     Follow the hints (No face found / Move closer / Scanning 1/5 ... 5/5).
  d) Check the details match > Confirm Check-In. The record shows Inside.
  e) Later: Scan Face to Check Out > Start Scan.
     It should show IZZAH TEST and "Match xx%". Confirm Check-Out.
  f) Visitor Records: IZZAH TEST shows "Checked Out" and badge "Face in/out".


5. ACCURACY TEST (for your report)
----------------------------------
  1. Check in two people by face (you + coworker), both stay inside.
  2. Do about 10 check-out scans of each person: different light, angles,
     glasses on/off. If VISTA shows the right person, note the score and press
     Not This Person (so they stay inside). If it shows the wrong person,
     press Not This Person too.
  3. Also scan someone who is NOT checked in: it should say "No match found".
  4. Write down the "Match xx%" for each scan (right person vs wrong person).
     A good result: right-person scores clearly above wrong-person scores.
     VISTA suggests a match only at 65% or more AND 8 points above the next
     person (same rule as the Cloud Functions version).
     (Scores are also kept on the phone: in the browser console run
      FaceLocal.logs() to see them.)
  5. When done, check out the test visitors (this deletes their face data).


6. LIMITATIONS (say these in the presentation)
----------------------------------------------
  - Same phone and browser only; another guard phone cannot check out.
  - Anyone with that phone can read the saved templates.
  - Photo / screen spoofing is not prevented; the guard must always confirm.
  - Accuracy depends on light, angle, masks, glasses; results with 2 people are
    an early indication, not proof.
  - For real use: CDC approval (PDPA, biometric data) + the Cloud Functions
    version on Blaze.
